# Feedback log — pitch page review

**Round 1 · 15 September 2026.** Three reviewers, all shown the client overview page at `ryvo-client-overview.vercel.app`.

> Kept for testimonials, positioning, and the product ideas that came out of it. Direct quotes preserved in the original language.

---

# 1. Martim Gabriel — friend, real-estate-minded

**Verdict: strong. Concept and value framing landed; presentation is the weak point.**

## What worked

> "tá muito bem feito"
> "os charts e informação tá boa"
> "gostei muito do slide to your own numbers"
> "o conceito tá incrível em termos de fazes muito por eles em termos de um preço muito valorizado para um business — e mostraste isso"

**The ROI slider is the standout feature, for the second time.** José independently pointed at the same element and called it *"a big rock que tens que passar."* Two of three reviewers singled out the same thing without prompting.

> "language change tá porreiro mas já tava à espera disso"

Worth noting: multilingual reads as table stakes to a young, technical audience. **It is not a differentiator in the pitch** — it is only a differentiator to an agency that has actually lost a foreign buyer at 7am.

## 🔴 The criticism, and it is the useful part

> "acho que podia ter mais no website se fores apresentar, por isso em termos de apeal"
> "mas tá um pouco tipo like blank and bland em termos de website"

**The page is under-designed for what it is asking.** The substance is right; the visual presence is not. This is the page that carries the first impression for eighteen cold emails and every prospect afterwards.

**Action:** treat this as a real finding, not a friend being picky. Worth a design pass before the next outreach round.

## 🟡 The product observation worth taking seriously

> "muitos clientes, por exemplo vamos dizer os nossos pais… o pessoal tem muito dinheiro para gastar numa casa… não vai tar a gastar o seu tempo e decisão valiosa com um AI"

**A buyer spending €1.5M will not make that decision through a chatbot, and will resent being asked to.**

His own resolution of it, which is correct and is already the design:

> "então fazer no aspeto of what you say: agencies demoram a responder of course. Mas ter aí o AI para fazer a introdução de opções até o agent possa full on ter a conversa"

**The AI is the introduction, not the relationship.** It answers fast, gathers the basics, and hands to a human before anything that matters. That is exactly what the Concierge does — but the pitch page does not say it clearly enough for a reader to reach that conclusion on their own.

**Action:** make the hand-off explicit in the pitch. *"The AI opens the door. Your agent closes."* That answers the objection before it is raised, and it is the truthful description of the product.

## 🔵 His expansion idea — logged, not adopted

He described a much larger thesis, offered to explain properly in person:

- Move beyond residential into **commercial, land, and investment** real estate
- An AI that tells investors **which land is worth buying and why** — doing the analysis an agency currently does by hand
- At that level: *"podes charge 10000 ao mês"*, possibly plus commission
- Then work **backwards** into the agency's own clients — investors, private equity, developers — since several of them need the same capability in reverse
- *"your clients are giving you more clients and your database is just an infinite loop of clients"*

**Honest assessment:** the instinct about who has budget is right, and the reverse-integration idea is genuinely clever. But it describes a different company with a different product, and it rests on analytical capability (valuation, land assessment) that does not exist and is much harder than conversation handling.

**Not a plan. A direction worth hearing out in person once there is one paying client.** File under §5, revisit at that point.

He also noted he had considered something similar and assumed it required building your own model — worth knowing that the *"you need to train your own AI"* misconception is what stops most people attempting this. **That misconception is part of the moat.**

# 2. Evelyn — owner, Sastrería Manuel Fernández · 🟢 testimonial-usable

**Verdict: unreserved positive. The closest thing to a real client voice in this round.**

> "Me gusta mucho!! Está súper bien presentado, se entiende fácil y se ve muy profesional. La idea está muy bien planteada y visualmente queda genial. Creo que transmite perfectamente lo que buscan las inmos"
>
> "El color todo me gusta"

## Why this one carries the most weight

She is a **business owner**, not a technical reviewer, and she is Spanish-speaking — which is the actual target profile. Her read directly contradicts Martim's on presentation.

**The two readings are not in conflict — they are two audiences.** Martim is young, online, and design-literate; he compares the page to modern SaaS sites. Evelyn compares it to what a real agency owner is used to seeing, and finds it professional and clear.

**Conclusion: the page works for its target and could be sharper for a design-literate reader.** Do not redesign for Martim's audience at the cost of Evelyn's.

**The phrase to keep:** *"transmite perfectamente lo que buscan las inmos."* That is close to a testimonial and she is the right kind of person to have said it.

**Action:** ask permission before using her words publicly. She has been actively helping with introductions, so the relationship supports the ask.

# 3. José Vale — father, owner of the operating company, 20+ years in business

**Verdict: endorsed, with a clear instruction on what actually closes.**

> "Mas parabéns pelo projeto, está top! E sim, os endinheirados não esperam…"

Then, pointing directly at the ROI calculator showing €48,000 commission on one sale against €14,400 of annual investment:

> "Esta é a big rock que tens que passar, mais nada!"

## What he is saying

**Stop selling the technology. Sell the arithmetic.** One recovered deal pays for three years of the complete system. That is the argument, and everything else is supporting material.

He also confirmed the core premise independently — *"os endinheirados não esperam"* — wealthy buyers do not wait. That is the whole basis for a concierge that answers in seconds.

**He is the most commercially experienced person in this round and he pointed at one thing.** Take the note.

# 4. What comes out of this

## The pattern across all three

| Reviewer | Singled out |
|---|---|
| Martim | The ROI slider |
| José | The ROI calculator |
| Evelyn | Presentation and clarity overall |

**Two of three independently pointed at the same element.** The ROI calculator is the strongest thing on the page and should be the centrepiece, not a feature partway down.

## Actions

1. 🔴 **Lead with the ROI calculator.** Two independent reviewers, one of them a business owner of twenty years, pointed at it unprompted.
2. 🔴 **Make the hand-off explicit** — *"the AI opens the door, your agent closes"* — to pre-empt Martim's high-value-buyer objection before a prospect raises it.
3. 🟡 **Design pass on the page**, but for polish rather than a rebuild. Evelyn's reaction says the foundation is right.
4. 🟡 **Ask Evelyn for permission** to quote *"transmite perfectamente lo que buscan las inmos."*
5. 🟢 **De-emphasise multilingual in the pitch.** It reads as expected to anyone under thirty. Keep it as a demo moment, not a headline.
6. 🔵 **Hear Martim out properly**, in person, once there is a paying client. Not before.

## What was NOT tested

All three reviewers are friendly parties. **None is a real estate agency owner deciding whether to spend €650 a month.** The feedback is useful on clarity and framing, and proves nothing about willingness to pay.

**That still only comes from a prospect.**
